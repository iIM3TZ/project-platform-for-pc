# Product pictures

Pictures of the parts, served at `/images/products/<file>` and set as each product's `image_url`
(in `server/db/seed.sql`, or picked in the admin page).

The product photos are the makers' official pictures, used with their permission. Some were taken from the
makers' sites, others from the same official pictures on store pages (Amazon.sa and others).
Do not add pictures taken from Google, other shops or reviews.
